About

Bridging theory and practice

Tech Economist makes secondary data systems, economic analysis and technology-driven research accessible and meaningful. Datasets are scattered, methods are often applied mechanically, and interpretation suffers. We organise the data, connect it with economic reasoning, and build free tools that do the heavy lifting.

The platform is rooted in Tamil Nadu and built for agricultural research across India and beyond.

  • Methods from peer-reviewed literature
  • Indian context: DES, APEDA, MOSPI
  • From UG classrooms to PhD research
  • AI-assisted, human-verified
